About

A technical writer running a small, focused studio.

I've spent the last five years working with cybersecurity and technology content, turning complex technical concepts into clear and useful content.

Most of that time has been spent next to engineers, product managers and security researchers. I've written blog programs for security vendors, rebuilt product documentation, interviewed customers for case studies, and helped founders get their ideas out of their heads and onto LinkedIn.

Along the way I saw the same problem again and again. Companies with excellent products were publishing content that was either too technical for buyers or too vague for engineers. Large content agencies often made it worse. Writers rotated, briefs were thin, and the client's own engineers ended up rewriting the drafts.

Clearframe exists to fix that. It gives technology companies a specialist who understands the subject, writes clearly, and stays on the account. You talk to the person writing your content.

Right now the studio is me. As the work grows, I'll bring in a small number of specialist writers I trust, and I'll stay involved in every project as editor and point of contact.

How I work

I start by understanding the product well enough to explain it to a smart person outside the field. I check facts, cite sources, and send drafts to your subject matter experts before anything is published. I use AI tools for research support where they help, and I review and write the final work myself.

I'm a good fit for teams who care about accuracy and want content their own engineers would share.
